4) Packaging & Handling

Safe & Secure Packaging Each order is packaged with utmost care based on product type, quantity, and delivery distance.

All metal items are packed with anti-rust bags or silica gel packs or cotton box where necessary.

5) Order Consolidation

For multiple products in one order:

  • We combine items to reduce shipping waste and improve efficiency. Heavy items are separated from fragile ones inside the same carton using internal partitions.
  • All hardware items are packed with protective materials to avoid transit damage. Fragile items include extra cushioning and tamper-proof seals.

6) Order Tracking Details

Once shipped, tracking details will be shared via email/SMS/Whatsapp. You can track your shipment through our website or the courier partner's site.

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive:

You can track your shipment in three ways:

10) Failed Deliveries

A delivery attempt will be made up to 3 times. If the order is undelivered due to:

  • Incorrect address
  • Customer unavailability
  • Refused delivery
  • Phone not reachable
  • Unserviceable PIN code

...the package will be returned to us. Re-delivery will require a new shipping charge to be paid by the customer.

How to Avoid Failed Deliveries

  • Double-check your address and PIN code
  • Ensure your phone number is reachable
  • Choose a delivery slot when someone is available
